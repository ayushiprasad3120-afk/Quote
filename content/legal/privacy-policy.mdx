We collect information you provide directly — such as your name, contact details, ZIP code, and coverage
interests — when you use our forms. We use this information to connect you with licensed insurance agents
and carrier partners, to operate and improve the site, and to comply with legal obligations. We do not sell
your personal information. We may share it with the licensed agents and partners helping you compare
coverage, and with service providers who support our operations under confidentiality obligations.
