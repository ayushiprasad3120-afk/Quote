If you've shopped for an ACA Marketplace plan, you've seen Bronze, Silver, Gold, and Platinum tiers — and
it's a common misconception that these describe the quality of doctors or hospitals in a plan's network.
They don't. Metal tiers describe cost-sharing: how the total cost of your care is split between your
monthly premium and your out-of-pocket costs when you actually use care.

## The general trade-off

Bronze plans usually have the lowest monthly premium and the highest out-of-pocket costs when you need
care. Platinum plans flip that: the highest premium, but the lowest costs when you use services. Silver
and Gold sit in between, and Silver plans are also the tier where additional cost-sharing reductions may
apply for eligible households.

## Choosing a tier

The right tier usually comes down to how often you expect to use healthcare. Someone who rarely visits a
doctor might prioritize a lower monthly premium, while someone managing an ongoing condition might come out
ahead with a higher-premium plan that lowers costs at the point of care. A licensed agent can help you run
that math based on your specific situation.
