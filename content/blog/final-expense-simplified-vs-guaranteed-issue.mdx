Final expense policies are typically issued one of two ways: simplified issue or guaranteed issue. Knowing
the difference can help you understand what to expect during the application process.

## Simplified issue

Simplified issue policies ask a short set of health questions but skip the medical exam. Because the
carrier is underwriting based on your answers, approval is often faster than traditional life insurance,
though certain health conditions can still affect eligibility or premium.

## Guaranteed issue

Guaranteed issue policies ask no health questions and require no exam at all — approval is close to
automatic. The trade-off is usually a higher premium relative to the coverage amount, and many guaranteed
issue policies include a graded benefit period, meaning the full death benefit may not apply if the
policyholder passes away from natural causes within the first two to three years.

## Which one fits

Generally, simplified issue is worth exploring first if you're in reasonably good health, since it often
costs less for the same coverage amount. Guaranteed issue exists as a safety net for people who might not
qualify otherwise. A licensed agent can help you figure out which path applies to your situation.
